The Bloodbath

Make your own bloodbath!

Seed: replays are exact only on the same version and setup. Export the log to keep a match.

By default everyone is solo. Add teams, then pick a team for each contestant from their dropdown. Tap a circle to choose a photo: use the image on your clipboard, or browse for a file. The Background image button works the same way. Photos and settings are saved on this device only.

Abilities use the tabletop scale (1 to 20, modifier = (score − 10) ÷ 2). 💪 STR sets weapon damage · 🏃 DEX sets accuracy, evasion, initiative and escape · 🛡️ CON sets max HP and resists infection · 📖 INT helps crits, evasion and coordination · 🧠 WIS helps evasion and crits, finds water, dodges hazards and guards supplies · 💬 CHA forms alliances · ❤ starting health · 💧 water is rounds until thirst · 🍖 food is rounds until hunger · bottles, rations and medkits are used automatically. Traits and jobs add modifiers, factions help coordination.

Optional rules
Solo events

☑ Enable/disable · Icon and Text appear in the log · % is the chance weight (higher means more likely to happen, one event per contestant) · Effect determines what happens to the contestant. Use {a} for the contestant's name and {w} for the weapon name.

Duo events

Use {a} for the initiator and {b} for the target. This event only triggers when two contestants cross paths.

Disasters

Enabled disasters strike at random. Any disaster can also be triggered by hand during a match.

Victim line: The text shown for each person hit (use {a} for their name). N: How many people it targets (0 means everyone, if applicable).

Traits and jobs

Traits add modifiers to a contestant (up to 3 each); a job is one extra role with the same kind of modifiers. Coord raises joint-attack chance, betray raises betrayal chance, ally raises alliance chance, heal is a Medic-style chance to patch up a groupmate. Contestants in the same faction coordinate better.

Combat fields are added to the 1 to 10 score each ability gives. Damage shifts the weapon's damage range and crit multiplier. Speed feeds accuracy, evasion and initiative. Wits feeds evasion and crit chance. Intellect feeds crit chance and evasion. Hover a field for its tooltip.

Status effects

Statuses last a number of rounds. Use them from the event and disaster effect dropdowns, or let fights cause bleeding. Damage, Speed, Wits and Intellect here are added to the same 1 to 10 scores as on traits. HP loss is a percentage of max HP per round (negative heals). High CON allows a save to resist. Grieving is special: its duration is ignored, because grief ends by revenge or by a per-round recovery chance.

Relationships

Lovers seek each other, join forces and grieve; if a lover is killed, the survivor swears a vendetta. Rivals and enemies hunt each other and make betrayal far more likely, and enemies of a target may join an attack on them. Open this panel again after renaming contestants to refresh the names.
