The Bloodbath

Make your own bloodbath!

By default everyone is solo. Add teams, then pick a team for each contestant from their dropdown. Tap a circle to add a photo. Photos and settings are saved on this device only.

Abilities use the tabletop scale (1 to 20, modifier = (score − 10) ÷ 2). 💪 STR is melee power · 🏃 DEX is speed and escaping · 🫀 CON soaks damage and resists infection · 📖 INT helps coordination and tactics · 🧠 WIS finds water, dodges hazards and guards supplies · 💬 CHA forms alliances · ❤ starting health · 💧 water is rounds until thirst · 🍖 food is rounds until hunger · bottles, rations and medkits are used automatically. Traits and jobs add modifiers, factions help coordination.

Rules and events

Edit, disable, remove or add events. Use {a} and {b} for names and {w} for a weapon. Fights, alliances and betrayals are always built in.

Disasters

Enabled disasters strike at random. Any disaster can also be triggered by hand during a match.

Traits and jobs

Traits add modifiers to a contestant (up to 3 each); a job is one extra role with the same kind of modifiers. Coord raises joint-attack chance, betray raises betrayal chance, ally raises alliance chance, heal is a Medic-style chance to patch up a groupmate. Contestants in the same faction coordinate better.

Status effects

Statuses last a number of rounds. Use them from the event and disaster effect dropdowns, or let fights cause bleeding.

Relationships

Lovers seek each other, join forces and grieve. Rivals and enemies hunt each other and make betrayal far more likely. Open this panel again after renaming contestants to refresh the names.
